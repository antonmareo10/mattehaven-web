Delivery & returns

Delivery

Prints are made to order with your name and time, checked by hand and printed by our print partner in Europe or the UK, as close to you as possible. Delivery is included in the price, and Etsy shows the estimated delivery date for your address at checkout. Etsy sends you the tracking number when your print ships.

For Christmas delivery, order before the date shown on your race's listing.

Returns

Each print is personalised, so it cannot be returned just because you changed your mind. If it arrives damaged, or something on it is wrong, write to us with a photo and your order number and we will reprint it or refund you.

Contact

Write to us through our Etsy shop.
