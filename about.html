About MatteHaven

MatteHaven is a small independent studio in Madrid. Behind it is Antonio, who loves the mountains, races and the outdoors, and who thinks a finish line deserves more than a medal in a drawer. We make official finisher prints for trail running and cycling races: the real course, drawn in 3D on the real relief, with each finisher's name and time.

How the prints are made

Every map starts from real data: the relief from public elevation models (Copernicus, IGN and other national mapping agencies), the landscape from aerial and satellite imagery, and the route from the race's official GPS track. The summits, the villages and the start and finish are named on the map, so you can find the climb that hurt the most.

Every print is checked by hand against the official course and results before it is printed on premium matte paper by our production partner, as close to you as possible.

Working with races

Official prints are made together with each race's organisers. The race sends one email to its finishers, and we take care of the rest: design, preview pages, printing, delivery and customer service.
